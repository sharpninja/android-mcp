`★ Insight ─────────────────────────────────────`
Two of the findings share one root cause: `outcome_unknown` is reachable from both registration and cleanup, and `SEND_INPUT` carries no generation on the wire. A state or a message that can be entered from two different histories needs a discriminator, otherwise later events are interpreted against the wrong history. Adding `uncertain_phase` to the subscription record and stating that raw input binds to the current generation are both one-field fixes.

**Uncertainties and limitations of this pass.**

- **U1 keepalive.** `RemoteControlProtocol.cs` line 36 defines `LogStreamKeepAliveCategory` for idle log streams. The design never mentions ARC log keepalive. Whether the upstream client depends on keepalive entries for liveness cannot be determined from the supplied files.
